# ARIA CAD workspace

CAD files use millimetres and stable component IDs from
`docs/ARIA-HW-001.md`.

- `parts/` — verified individual component models and keep-outs
- `assemblies/` — robot packaging and subassemblies
- `pcb/` — PCB outlines, origins, mounting constraints, and populated-board STEP
- `drawings/` — dimensioned mechanical drawings

Do not promote downloaded community models without checking their critical
dimensions against manufacturer data or a real sample.
