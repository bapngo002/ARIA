# CAD review holding area

These files are preserved because they are unique or nominal, but they are **not** approved manufacturing CAD.

- `01-raspberry-pi-5-with-official-cooler_UNVERIFIED-MISMATCH.dwg`: includes an Official Active Cooler, while the purchased cooler was recorded as Smraza.
- `16-yd-esp32-s3-n16r8/`: the pinout evidence identifies the purchased PCB family as the 44-pin dual-USB-C YD-ESP32-S3 N16R8. The replacement 2D/3D CAD matches that family's nominal geometry, but tight enclosure dimensions still require measurement of the delivered board.
